# ALL MAINTENANCE — Website adapted from the 8B Design Studio template

This is the complete one-page site, adapted to **All Maintenance | Flower Installation** with the company's supplied brand mark and landscaping/service content. It is an HTML/CSS/JS website, so no npm installation, React, or build command is required.

## Open the website

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. The photographs and logo are included locally in `assets/`.
3. An internet connection is needed for the original template's GSAP/ScrollTrigger/Lenis motion libraries and external web fonts. The page content and photographs remain readable if those libraries are unavailable.

## Project structure

- `index.html`: all page content, responsive layout styles, template scenes, animations, navigation and forms.
- `assets/`: supplied company logo and landscaping image assets, including image crops for different sections.
- `LICENSE`: original MIT license from the source design template.
- `TEMPLATE_CREDITS.md`: original design template's attributions.

## Updated content and sections

The page includes: animated landing hero, introduction, horizontal photo gallery, benefits wheel, all five landscaping service groups, four-step process, photo collage, outdoor care articles, testimonial layout, FAQ, free-estimate form and footer.

## Before publishing

- **Reviews:** cards are explicitly labeled examples, **not customer reviews**. Replace these with genuine approved testimonials.
- **Photographs:** included imagery is illustrative and not verified photographs of All Maintenance's completed customer projects. Replace with authentic company photos for your best result.
- **Contact:** no actual business email address, phone number or city was provided. These have intentionally not been invented. Add the correct company contact details and receiving mechanism before publishing.
- **Form:** the free-estimate form checks the inputs and prepares/copies a message. It does **not** send to a server. Connect the form to your own email provider / Formspree / backend before going live.
- **Coverage:** business service location remains unspecified. Update the service-area paragraph in the FAQ when confirmed.
- **Analytics and SEO:** update social sharing image, canonical URL, business schema, metadata and tracking for your production domain if required.

## Editing

Open `index.html` in a code editor. Brand colors are defined in the `--color-*` variables and custom adaptations are near the bottom of the `<head>` under `maintenance-custom`.

Animations are derived from the supplied MIT-licensed 8B template. Credits are retained in the footer and in `TEMPLATE_CREDITS.md`.
